<p><b>kwbooktrack</b> is an offline tracker for your personal book and movie collection. No account, no ads, no analytics: everything stays on your device and the app never asks for internet access.</p>

<p><b>Book library</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Author, ISBN, page count, publisher, year, genre, language, series, binding, condition, annotation</li>
<li>Cover from the gallery or camera, purchase date and price, gift / lent / sold flags</li>
<li>Search by title, author, ISBN and tag, filters by status and purchase period, grid or list view</li>
<li>Scan an ISBN barcode with the camera to fill the card quickly</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Movie collection</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Director, cast, country, year, genres, duration, link to the movie page</li>
<li>Watch date, rating, comments, tags</li>
<li>The same search, filters and grid/list switch as for books</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Reading log and progress</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Reading sessions with current page, dates and time spent</li>
<li>Progress in pages and percent, pages left</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Notes, quotes and reviews</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Notes, quotes and reviews with optional page reference and attached photos</li>
<li>One notes list with search and a type filter</li>
<li>Markdown in reviews and notes</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Statistics and calendar</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Pages read, books finished, books bought and money spent — per month and per year</li>
<li>Movies watched and time spent watching</li>
<li>Reading calendar by day: book covers or pages per day</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Import and export</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Backup and restore as a ZIP archive with CSV, covers and photos</li>
<li>Native Tellico format (.tc) import and export</li>
<li>Import of Tellico XML and CSV exports and of libraries from other book trackers</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Look and feel</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Light and dark theme, or follow the system</li>
<li>Russian and English interface</li>
</ul>
