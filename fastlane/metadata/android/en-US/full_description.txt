<p><b>kwbooktrack</b> is an offline manager for your personal book and movie collection: a home library catalogue, reading log, notes and statistics. No account, no ads, no analytics, no internet permission — everything stays on your device.</p>

<p>More than a reading tracker: it is a full collection catalogue, so what sits on your shelves can be described as thoroughly as in library software and exported to a desktop catalogue.</p>

<p><b>Collection catalogue</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Books: author, original title, subtitle, ISBN, page count, publisher, year, print year, print pages, genre, language and original language, translator, series and series number, binding, condition, annotation</li>
<li>Owner flags: gift, lent out, sold; purchase date, price and place</li>
<li>Movies: director, cast, country, year, genres, duration, link to the movie page, downloaded flag</li>
<li>Cover from the gallery or camera, rating, arbitrary extra fields</li>
<li>Tags for books and movies, shared across the collection</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Search and filters</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Search by title, author, ISBN, director, actor and tags</li>
<li>Filters by status (to read, unread, reading, finished, abandoned, purchased) and by purchase period</li>
<li>Grid or list view with covers, match counter</li>
<li>ISBN barcode scanning with the camera to fill a card quickly</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Tellico compatibility</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Import and export in the native Tellico format (.tc) — move a collection between the phone and the Tellico desktop catalogue in both directions</li>
<li>Import of tellico.xml and Tellico CSV exports</li>
<li>Migrate libraries from other book trackers without losing fields: covers, notes and photos come along with the records</li>
<li>Choose which collection to export — books or movies</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Reading log and progress</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Reading sessions with current page, dates and time spent</li>
<li>Progress in pages and percent, pages left</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Notes, quotes and reviews</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Notes, quotes and reviews with an optional page reference and attached photos</li>
<li>One list with search and a type filter</li>
<li>Markdown in reviews and notes</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Statistics and calendar</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Pages read, books finished, books bought and money spent — per month and per year</li>
<li>Movies watched and time spent watching</li>
<li>Reading calendar by day: book covers or pages per day</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Backups</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Export and restore as a ZIP archive with CSV, covers and photos</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Look and feel</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Light and dark theme, or follow the system</li>
<li>Russian and English interface</li>
</ul>

<p>Suited to cataloguing a home library, second-hand and antiquarian books, a film collection, keeping a reading diary, and moving a Tellico catalogue onto a phone.</p>
