<p><b>kwbooktrack</b> is an offline tracker for your personal book collection. No account, no ads, no analytics: everything stays on your device.</p>

<p><b>Library</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Books with author, ISBN, page count, publisher, genre, language, series, binding and condition</li>
<li>Cover photo, purchase date and price, gift / lent / sold flags</li>
<li>Search by title, author or ISBN, filters (to read, reading, finished, abandoned, purchased) and grid or list view</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Reading log</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Reading sessions with current page, start and finish dates, time spent</li>
<li>Progress in pages and percent, pages left, and honest warnings when an old backup has broken data</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Notes, quotes and reviews</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Notes, quotes and reviews with optional page reference and attached photos</li>
<li>Markdown rendering for reviews</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Statistics and calendar</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Pages read and books finished per month and per year</li>
<li>Total money spent on books, reading calendar by day</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Import and export</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Backup and restore as a ZIP archive with CSV and covers</li>
<li>Native Tellico format (.tc) import and export</li>
<li>Import of Tellico XML and CSV exports, and of libraries exported from other book trackers</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Other</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Scan an ISBN barcode with the camera to pre-fill book details</li>
<li>No internet permission, no crash reporting, no tracking</li>
</ul>
